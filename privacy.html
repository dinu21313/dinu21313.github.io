Privacy Policy - Solider Cat XD

Last updated: October 2026

1. What data is used

The app accesses only the owner's own TikTok account, through the TikTok API: basic profile info, the list of the owner's own videos and their public statistics, and the ability to upload the owner's own videos.

2. Where data is stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not shared, sold or published.

3. Other users

The app does not collect, store or process personal data of any other person.

4. Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the TikTok app settings.

5. Contact

Send a message to @solidercatxd on TikTok.
